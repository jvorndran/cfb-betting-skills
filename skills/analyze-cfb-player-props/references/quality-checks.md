# Player-Prop Quality Checks

- Verify player, team, opponent, position, game, and namespaced IDs; check transfers and namesakes.
- Verify exact stat, period, direction, threshold, attached price, provider, observation time, and settlement rules.
- Set an `as_of` time and exclude the target game and later outcomes.
- Confirm the sample boundary reflects the current role and uses consistent season-type and garbage-time treatment.
- Do not relabel FBS usage as snaps, routes, targets, depth, or availability.
- Separate opportunity from efficiency and identify which drives the result.
- Check current status, role, and teammate dependencies with appropriately scoped sources.
- Preserve source limitations and disclose stale, inaccessible, capped, tier-blocked, or ambiguous evidence.
- Avoid double-counting box stats, usage, success, PPA, WEPA, and play summaries from the same underlying plays.
- Recompute price conversions and compare the exact threshold—not a nearby market.
- Stress-test committee roles, snap limitations, quarterback changes, weather, and outlier production.
- Use `insufficient_evidence` when identity, role, availability, price, rules, or sample scope could materially reverse the conclusion.
