# Individual matchup presentation contract

Use this reference only after the user selects a native interactive visualization or persistent report.

## Objective

Make the matchup thesis, decisive uncertainty, and next useful observation understandable before the source ledger. The presentation is a research interface, not a pick sheet.

## Recommended order

1. decision-first summary;
2. up to four evidence-backed metric cards;
3. game identity and cutoff;
4. executive matchup summary;
5. continuity and independent team snapshots;
6. unit-versus-unit interactions with failure modes;
7. personnel, availability, environment, and scheduling context;
8. qualitative game scripts and confirmation triggers;
9. prior-meeting portability, counterevidence, and unknowns; and
10. claims, sources, downstream statuses, and validation.

Use a `research state` rather than inventing a betting decision when no market analysis was requested. Summarize routed results without merging them into `matchup_context`.

## Visuals

Prefer two to four evidence-supported visuals. Good defaults are continuity, a key-mechanism decomposition, a freshness or role table, and a quote comparison supplied by routed market work. Give every visual a nearby interpretation and limitation.

Do not invent an edge score, confidence gauge, projection, or decorative metric. Do not chart qualitative scenarios on a numeric axis or arrange different-provider quotes as a time series. Use a table when data is categorical or sparse.

## Evidence and status

- Keep the report readable without interaction and retain relative references to structured records when persistent output is selected.
- Show `unknown`, `limited`, `questionable`, and `confirmed` distinctly.
- Explain why historical efficiency or a prior meeting is or is not portable.
- Start `validation_status` at `unvalidated`; only `$validate-cfb-research` may change it to `pass`, `warn`, or `fail`.
- Surface validator blockers without softening them and never force action to make the presentation appear useful.
