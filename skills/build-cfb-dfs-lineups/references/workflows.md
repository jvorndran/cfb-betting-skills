# Common CFB DFS Workflows

Combine the modules that fit the slate, contest, and user objective.

## Analysis modules

### Slate and contest

Confirm included games, lock structure, scoring, roster constraints, payout shape, and whether late swap changes the value of flexibility.

### Projection review

Inspect the opportunity and efficiency assumptions behind supplied projections. Compare floor, median, and ceiling rather than treating one point estimate as truth.

### Role and availability

Identify starters, committees, replacements, teammate dependencies, and late-news risk. Distinguish official status from reporting and projections.

### Game environment

Evaluate pace, expected play volume, team scoring environment, concentration of production, opponent strengths, weather, and blowout sensitivity.

### Salary and ownership

Compare outcome range with salary and expected ownership. Treat ownership as a sourced estimate, not an observed fact before lock.

### Correlation and leverage

Consider quarterback-receiver pairings, bring-backs, rushing concentration, opposing game environments, duplicated assumptions, and contest-specific leverage. Correlation is strategy, not a provider rule.

### Portfolio

When multiple lineups are requested, examine uniqueness, exposure, concentration, late-swap paths, and whether every lineup expresses the same fragile thesis.

## Useful workflow shapes

- **Cash-style analysis:** Emphasize stable role, floor, salary efficiency, and failure risk.
- **Tournament analysis:** Emphasize ceiling, ownership, leverage, correlation, and paths to first-place outcomes.
- **Single-game analysis:** Focus on multiplier rules, game-script clusters, duplication risk, and conditional correlations.
- **Late-swap review:** Reassess availability, remaining salary, ownership information, and unlocked alternatives at the applicable time.
- **Lineup validation:** Ignore strategic appeal until every hard provider constraint passes.

Stop when another metric or source cannot change a projection range, classification, exposure, or construction decision.
