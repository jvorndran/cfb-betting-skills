# DFS Quality Checks

## Analysis

- Verify provider, slate, contest, lock time, scoring, and included games.
- Keep provider and CFBD player/game IDs separate.
- Distinguish observed data, supplied projections, supplied ownership, and assumptions.
- Check that historical inputs precede the decision cutoff and exclude target-slate outcomes.
- Do not relabel FBS usage as snaps, routes, projections, current depth, or availability.
- Confirm current status and role with appropriately scoped sources.
- Preserve catalog limitations and disclose stale, inaccessible, capped, tier-blocked, or ambiguous evidence.
- Avoid double-counting related box, usage, success, PPA, WEPA, and play-derived metrics.
- Match floor, ceiling, leverage, and correlation judgments to the contest objective.
- Do not produce lineups unless the user requested them.

## Requested lineups

- Recompute salary, slots, eligibility, slate membership, team/game rules, locks, and user constraints from authoritative rows.
- Verify questionable, out, ineligible, off-slate, and locked players using current evidence.
- Recompute portfolio exposures, uniqueness, stacks, and concentration.
- Fail invalid or unverifiable lineups; never present drafts as ready entries.
- Show alternatives for material availability or role uncertainty.
- Never upload or submit an entry.
